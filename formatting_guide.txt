1. The Header (Frontmatter)

This goes at the absolute top of the file. Only use one per post. If you put two, the site breaks.
YAML

---
title: Your Trash Opinion
published: 2026-08-22
description: Brief summary.
tags: [Visual Novel, Rant]
category: Reviews
draft: false
---

2. Basic Text

Don't overcomplicate it. Use Markdown.

    # Heading 1 (Don't use this, your title is already H1)

    ## Heading 2 (Use this for main sections)

    ### Heading 3 (Sub-sections)

    **Bold text**

    *Italic text*

    Inline text spoiler: :spoiler[your hidden degenerate thought]

5. Colored Warning Boxes (Admonitions)

Use these if you want to yell at your readers. Supported types: note, tip, important, warning, caution.
Markdown

:::warning[Skip This Route]
This character's route is a massive waste of 50 hours.
:::
