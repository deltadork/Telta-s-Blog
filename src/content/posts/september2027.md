This was a not very productive month, but at least I found something truly impressive that made me want to give more chances to modern visual novels which I tend to avoid. Main reason why I don't new VNs is that there are not as much plotge with eros these days, but also a lot of them just stink with lowbrow moege dialogues. Anyway, let's start.

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Dimension Totsu lovers</h2>
<img src="/files/rakuen/6aa6ebf5-8df6-4707-bcd3-eb8b1617f33c.png" width=npm run dev"500" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>  
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.85rem; color: #888; margin-top: 0;">Room that is impossible to leave without having sex</p>  

New release that everyone already played. I stubbornly ignored it, because it seemed like an average overhyped story. I would like to apologize to developers, because this is kino. Might be the most entertaining visual novel I have ever seen. It combines everything: lovey dovey romance, chuuni dynamic battles, edgy brutal world building, funny zoomer dialogues and much more. There is just nothing quite like it, despite the fact that it doesn't really invent anything new. Paradox. 
What people do not mention often is that soundtrack is pretty fire too, some of the battle ost has been stuck in my head for days, like this one:

The story itself is flawed, relies on conveniences, and true route isn't good (some people say it's a 神 route tho), but honestly? I don't even care. It's just a fun, fast paced story and I hope more VN developers will get inspired by it. 
There is also another reason for why I'm hyping this up so much. H-scenes. They're fucking awesome. Femdom h-scenes in mainstream VN isn't something you see often, if ever, so it was a pleasent surprised. Heroines are pretty hot and interesting, so I was excited every time while entering a new route.
Totsuraba didn't change my life, but it changed my view on VNs, so I'm thankful for that. We ALL will be waiting for the fandisc.

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Saiaku Naru Saiyaku Ningen Ni Sasasagu</h2>
<img src="/public/files/rakuen/6a9f0f13-67e9-4e90-b312-e424d597f3dc.png" width=npm run dev"500" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>  

Sasasagu finally got a steam release (that is also a remake). It's my favourite visual novel as of now, so I was waiting for this release eagerly. I've already wrote a review that you can [read here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4549300/My_Dearest_Direst_Disaster/). In short, I think it's a work that is impossible to tell in any other medium without losing much of the appeal, with almost perfect production values, strong writing and a compelling message. Everyone who knows japanese should read it.  

Steam release has new afterstory, and overall I'm very satisfied with what we've got. To be fair, :spoiler[I expected true ending's kuro to get an after story too, but it would be hard to implenet without ruining the ending so I can overlook it. Every afterstory was well written, but the best thing about them are the endings. I didn't cry, but I could. The tears were welling up, I swear. Also I cried many times when I've read the main story, so my dears dried up. Let's move on...]  
I have not reread the game to see all new CGs and changes, so I'll just state my opinion on the artstyle. It's not better than the old one. Yes it is more polished, and they fixed weird CGs. New readers probably won't mind it, but the fact that Kuro's eyes no longer shine like brilliants is a crime. 
<img src="/files/rakuen/New Project(7).jpg" width="500" style="display: block; margin: 24px auto;"/> 
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.85rem; color: #888; margin-top: 0;">New CG looks very good, I admit it</p>  
I would advice to read the fan port for the game first, and then skip through the steam version for new CGs and afterstories.  
The next VN from this writer that will get a PC release is Happy Route O Owarasete and I'm very excited for it. It's a meta kind of story, with fully voiced cast, inclucing protagonist, so I think it will be interesting.
