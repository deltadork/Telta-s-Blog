What's the point of reading nukige? There was a time when I couldn't understand why someone would read a bloated 10-hour-long porn game where you have to wait an hour to reach the actual deal, when doujinshi provide all the meat in the first minute. Well, there are many reasons why this medium is so good for porn, but the main one is that it's simply more immersive. The power of prose, so to speak. Voices and animations enhance the experience a lot, but the text is the core. There is not a single NTR doujin that hits as hard as an NTR visual novel where you spend 5 hours romancing your waifu just for an ugly bastard to steal her.  
Anyway, today I will showcase the 5 best visual novels I have played. They're all very different in terms of fetishes, but I feel there is at least one VN here that someone will enjoy. My favorite one will be at the end. Note that most of them are untranslated. Let's start.

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Seven Days: Mishiranu JK to Misshitsu Seikatsu</h2>

<img src="https://files.catbox.moe/bb1xs8.png" width="500" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;" alt="Seven Days" />

Debut work released in 2025 by independent developer Pome-ya. An ojisan and a high schooler. A mysterious room with no way out. A weird chibi girl giving out the orders. The premise is standard, but that's why it works. The relationship between the main characters develops very organically despite the relatively short run time (~4 hours). The plot/ecchi balance is extremely solid, and there is even some decent plot that keeps you motivated to read further.  

Another thing I want to mention is the style: a monochrome art style with lo-fi music, simplistic UI, and a bit of casual gameplay. It's just a very enjoyable VN to read from start to finish.  

In terms of H-scenes, I'd say it's solid. There is a urination fetish, a lot of humiliation, and cute reactions from the heroine. Personally, I'm not a fan of massive breasts, but the heroine has a certain charm anyway, so I didn't mind.  

Overall, I'd recommend it to anyone who doesn't mind unvoiced VNs and wants something with more substance than just pure porn.  

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Makai Tenshi Djibril 4</h2>

<img src="https://files.catbox.moe/3xer37.png" width="500" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;" alt="Makai Tenshi Djibril 4" />

Now we're talking about something spicy. I haven't read the other visual novels in this series, but Djibril 4 is completely standalone, so it doesn't matter. It's a henshin/magical girl parody with a focus on harem... and NTR. Yes, both. Surprisingly, it doesn't feel jarring since the atmosphere is overall lighthearted, and while the amount of NTR isn't small, it's mostly optional. The plot revolves around the protagonist and his three friends fighting against demons who want to force everyone to have sex. Don't question it.  

Since it's a henshin story, the structure is very episodic: a mesugaki demon and her friend cause trouble, the heroines try to save the day, walk into a trap, and get assaulted in the bad ending. After each arc, there is a screen where you can choose which H-scene you want to view right away.  

The heroines are fine, except for Aoi—the cute, black-haired tsundere featured in that footjob CG (extremely moe). Her NTR scenes are exceptionally hot. For some reason, most of them focus on her swallowing semen and giving blowjobs. I can totally see why the devs went that route. Aoi alone is reason enough to read this game.

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Bishoujo Mangekyou -Norowareshi Densetsu no Shoujo-</h2>

<img src="https://files.catbox.moe/3ppj8x.jpg" width="500" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;" alt="Bishoujo Mangekyou" />

Everyone knows the Biman series, but I still have to mention it. In my opinion, Biman 1 is the ultimate "baby's first nukige." It features some of the best art in the entire genre, animated H-scenes, a cute tsundere heroine, and a simple yet engaging story. The ecchi scenes are mostly focused on vanilla sex, with just a hint of femdom. The protagonist is unhinged, so self-inserting is tough, but this is the kind of twisted romance you should observe from a distance anyway.
