### Seven Days: Mishiranu JK to Misshitsu Seikatsu
![Seven Days](https://files.catbox.moe/bb1xs8.png)
Debute work released in 2025 by independent developer Pome-ya. Ojisan and a high schooler. Mysterious room with no way out. Weird loli giving out the orders. The premise is standart, but that's why it's good. Relationsheep between main characters develops very organically despite the relatively short run time (~4 hours), plot/ecchi balance is extremely good, there is even some decent plot that makes you motivated to read it further.  

Other thing I want to mentinon is the style. Monochrome artstyle with lo-fi kind of music, simplistic UI, a bit of casual gameplay. It's just a very enjoyable VN to read from start to finish.  

In terms of h-scenes I would say it's just good. There is some pissing fetish, a lot of humiliation, and cute reactions from the heroine. Personally, I'm not a fan of big breasts, but there is a certain charm to the heroine anyway so I didn't mind it.  

Overall I would recommend it to people who don't mind unvoiced VNs, and want something more than just porn.  

### Makai Tenshi Djibril 4
![Makai Tenshi Djibril 4](https://files.catbox.moe/3xer37.png)
Now we're talking about something spicy. I have not read other visual novel in this series, but Dijibril 4 is completely standalone so it doesn't matter. It's a henshin/magical girls parody, with a focus on harem... and NTR. Yes, both. Since it's a henshin story, the plot is very episodic. Loli demon and her friend do something bad - heroines try to save someone - heroines get in the trap and are raped in the bad ending. ### Bishoujo Mangekyou -Norowareshi Densetsu no Shoujo-
![Bishoujo Mangekyou -Norowareshi Densetsu no Shoujo-](https://files.catbox.moe/3ppj8x.jpg)
Everyone knows about biman series, but I still need to mention it. In my opinion, biman 1 is an ultimate baby's first nukige. It has some of the best art in the genre, animations in most h-scenes, cute tsundere heroine, and simple but interesting story. Protagonist is a bit crazy, so I imagine it would be hard to self insert, but this is a kind of twisted romance story you should watch from far apart anyway.
